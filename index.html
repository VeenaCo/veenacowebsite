Questions people ask

What does Veena do?

We help owner-managed businesses take care of repetitive customer messages, so you have more time for the conversations that need you. We set up WhatsApp automation and build and host websites.

What does the WhatsApp service do?

It answers common questions, handles enquiries, captures details, books appointments and guides customers to the next step.

What if a customer needs a real person?

The conversation is handed over to you, so a person steps in when it needs one.

Do I have to change how I work?

No. We don’t simply switch something on. Everything is set up around your services, your conversations and your way of working.

Do you build websites too?

Yes. We design and build your website, host it and look after changes, so your website and WhatsApp work together as one flow.

How do I pay?

There is a one-time setup fee, then a monthly retainer for the WhatsApp platform. Websites have a monthly fee that covers hosting and changes.

Who do you work with?

Owner-managed businesses such as clinics, salons, studios, tradespeople and local retailers, and any business looking for a calmer way to handle customer messages. We are based in South Africa and work with businesses across the globe.

How much does it cost?

It depends on your business, so message us on WhatsApp or email and we’ll talk it through.

What happens after I get in touch?

We follow four steps: understand, map, configure and connect, then handover. You can read them in how it works.
